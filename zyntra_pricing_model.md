# ZYNTRA Pricing Model

> **Last updated:** June 2, 2026
>
> **Pricing philosophy:** Pay only for the exams you deliver. No monthly waste. No annual lock-in.

---

## Core Concept

ZYNTRA uses a **credit-based pricing model**.

```
1 credit = 1 student taking 1 exam
```

Credits are consumed each time a student takes an exam. The number of credits consumed per session depends on which features are enabled for that exam.

---

## Credit Multipliers

Different features consume different amounts of credits per exam session.

| Feature | Credits Consumed | Description |
|:---|:---:|:---|
| Basic Exam (no proctoring) | **1 credit** | Standard exam delivery — question rendering, timer, submission, and scoring |
| AI Proctoring | **2 credits** | Real-time AI monitoring — gaze tracking, audio analysis, tab-switch detection, behaviour flagging |
| Biometric Verification | **+1 credit** | Add-on — facial recognition identity verification before exam start |
| Auto-Grading (AI Essays) | **+1 credit** | Add-on — NLP-powered essay evaluation, rubric-based scoring, plagiarism detection |
| Live Proctoring *(coming soon)* | **5 credits** | Human-supervised exam sessions with real-time intervention capability |

### Subscription credit policy

- Included credits are allocated at the start of each billing cycle.
- **Credits do not roll over.** Unused credits expire at the end of the billing month.
- Extra credits beyond the included amount are billed at the plan's extra-credit rate.
- Plans can be upgraded or downgraded at any time. Changes take effect at the next billing cycle.

## Mixing Subscriptions & Bundles

Institutions can use both a subscription plan and credit bundles simultaneously.

**Example:** A university on the **Institution plan** (10,000 credits/mo) purchases a **Bulk Bundle** (10,000 credits) for their annual finals week when they need 18,000 credits in a single month.

- Monthly credits: 10,000 (from subscription)
- Bundle credits: 8,000 consumed (from the 10,000 bundle)
- Total available: 20,000 — finals week covered
- Remaining bundle credits: 2,000 (valid for 12 months)

Credits from subscription plans are consumed first. Bundle credits are consumed only after monthly credits are exhausted.

## FAQ

**Q: What is a credit?**
A: 1 credit = 1 student taking 1 basic exam. Features like AI proctoring or auto-grading consume additional credits per session. See the Credit Multipliers table above for exact rates.

**Q: Do unused subscription credits roll over?**
A: No. Credits included in monthly subscription plans are allocated per billing cycle and expire at the end of that month. Credit bundles have their own validity period (90 days to 12 months).

**Q: Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?**
A: Yes. Plan changes take effect at the next billing cycle. You are never locked into a plan.

**Q: What currencies do you support?**
A: We offer regional pricing in USD, GBP, EUR, and NGN. Your currency is detected automatically or can be changed in billing settings.

**Q: Is there a free plan?**
A: Yes. The Starter plan is free forever with 50 credits per month. No credit card required.

**Q: Do you offer education pricing?**
A: Yes. Public schools, government institutions, and non-profit educational organisations get up to 40% off all plans and bundles. Contact sales to apply.

**Q: Is there a minimum commitment?**
A: Monthly subscription plans have no minimum commitment — cancel anytime. Enterprise plans require a 12-month minimum term. Credit bundles have no commitment beyond the purchase.

**Q: Can I use bundles without a subscription?**
A: Yes. Credit bundles are standalone purchases with no subscription required. Perfect for one-off exam events.
